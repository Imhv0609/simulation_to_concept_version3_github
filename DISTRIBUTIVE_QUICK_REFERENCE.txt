TEACHING CONCEPTS (6 concepts)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Understanding the Distributive Property
   - Core rule: a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c
   - Fundamental algebraic property

2. Dot Array Visualization
   - Visual with colored dots in rows and columns
   - Blue dots (a × b) + Green dots (a × c) = Total

3. Area Model Visualization
   - Rectangle split into two colored sections
   - Visual proof through geometric areas

4. Mental Math with Distributive Property
   - Practical applications for quick calculations
   - Break hard numbers into easy ones

5. Distributive Property with Subtraction
   - Extension: a × (b − c) = a × b − a × c
   - Works with both + and −

6. Why It Works - Mathematical Reason
   - Real-world analogy (bags of candies)
   - Conceptual understanding
